About us

FOODSHARE+

At FOODSHARE+, we are a community-driven platform committed to preventing food waste. We connect local restaurants, grocery stores, and food providers with those in need, ensuring that extra food is shared rather than discarded.

How it Works

How do we do to help?

If students have extra food, they can donate it by placing it in the provided refrigerator/storage/box and labeling it as halal or non-halal.

We also work with restaurants to manage their surplus food (for example, from bakeries).
